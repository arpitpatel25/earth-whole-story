T: In the early 13th century BC, Ramesses II of Egypt attacked the Hittite Empire of Muwatalli II on the Orontes River. It is the earliest pitched battle for which details of tactics and formations are known, and is believed to be the largest chariot battle ever fought, with 5,000 to 6,000 chariots in all.
K: More than 3,200 years ago, the armies of Egypt and the Hittites fought beside a river on what is today the border of Lebanon and Syria. Thousands of chariots took part. It is the oldest battle that we know the details of.
note: The source gives only the early 13th century BC. The year shown is approximate.

T: In 490 BCE the citizens of Athens, aided by Plataea, met a Persian force sent by King Darius I in his first attempt to subjugate Greece. The Greek army inflicted a crushing defeat on the more numerous Persians. It was a turning point in the Greco-Persian Wars.
K: In 490 BCE the huge Persian Empire sent an army to conquer Greece. The citizens of Athens met it at a place called Marathon and won, even though there were more Persians.
