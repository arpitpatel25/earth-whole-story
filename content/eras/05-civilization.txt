S: The Neolithic, or New Stone Age, saw farming, herding and settled village life arise independently in several parts of the world. The domestication of plants and animals began in separate places after the end of the last ice age, 11,700 years ago.

level: 1

src: Bronze Age
S: A society is counted as Bronze Age if it made bronze by smelting copper and alloying it with tin or other metals, or traded for bronze made elsewhere. In the Near East the Early Bronze Age is dated from 3300 BCE; on the Indian subcontinent it began about the same time with the Indus Valley Civilisation.
K: People learned to melt copper and mix it with tin or other metals to make bronze. Those who could not make it traded for it.
